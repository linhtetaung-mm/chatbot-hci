# Using the study chatbot

Use this with the information sheet provided by the research team. That sheet explains the study, consent, privacy, contacts and your appointment dates.

## Before you start

You need a phone or computer, an internet connection, your personal access code and the link from the team. Keep your code private. You do not need an OpenAI account or API key.

1. Open the link the team gave you for this session.
2. Enter your access code and press **Sign in**. Capital letters and the hyphen are optional.
3. Read the consent information. Ask the team if anything is unclear before agreeing.

## Each chat

1. Think of something mildly stressful from your day or week. Avoid real names, contact details and very serious or sensitive situations.
2. Use the slider to show how upset you feel, then press **Start chat**.
3. Type a message and press **Send**. Wait for the chatbot's reply before sending another. On a keyboard, Enter sends and Shift+Enter adds a new line.
4. Press **Finish chat** when you are done. If it is unavailable, the page explains whether you need another reply or more messages. You can still stop at any time.
5. Answer the three questions and press **Submit**. When the page says your answers are saved, you can close it.

Use the link and schedule supplied by the research team between and during your appointments. Use the final-session link only when instructed. For a task that the team asks you to do without AI, close the chatbot and complete it on your own.

## If something goes wrong

- **No reply or connection error:** keep this page open, check your internet connection, then press **Try again**. You do not need to copy and send the message a second time. Failed question submissions keep your choices on this page too. Unsaved text or choices may be lost if you reload or close the page.
- **Code does not work or chat is paused:** contact the research team using your information sheet. Do not use another person's code.
- **Using a shared device:** press **Sign out** when finished so the next person cannot open your chat.

## Stopping and getting help

Press **Stop** if you want to end the current chat. Stopping a chat does not delete records already saved. To withdraw or request deletion, contact the research team as described in your information sheet.

This is an AI chatbot, not a counsellor or emergency service. Chats are not monitored live. If you feel very distressed, stop and use the support contacts on your information sheet or screen. For an immediate emergency, seek urgent local help.
